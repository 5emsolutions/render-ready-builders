Golf Course Call Capture

The calls your shop misses are the ones worth the most.

How it works

Your main line does not change and your staff answer exactly as they do now. Our system only picks up what was already going to voicemail — after hours and when the counter is three deep. It answers common questions, takes tee time requests, and passes outing and event inquiries straight to your staff instead of letting them sit until Monday.

Text messages we send

Text messaging is used only to confirm a request and to alert course staff. We send no marketing, promotions, or advertising of any kind, and we never message a number that was purchased, rented, or supplied by a third party.

  • Booking confirmations to golfers. One message per call you place, sent only after you agree to it on the phone. Example: "Cypress Ridge — you're confirmed. Sat, Aug 22 at 7:20 AM. 2 players · 18 holes · Cart included. Confirmation CR-8F3K2. Reply STOP to opt out. Msg&data rates may apply."
  • Course information links to golfers. When a caller asks about dress code, rentals, rain checks, or outing packages, we text a link to that policy rather than reading it aloud.
  • Lead alerts to course staff. Named employees listed in the course's service agreement receive alerts when an outing or event inquiry comes in.

How you opt in

Consent is collected verbally, during a call you place to the course. Before the call ends you are asked:

"Before I let you go — can I text you a confirmation at the number you're calling from? You'll get one message confirming your tee time. You can reply STOP any time to opt out, and message and data rates may apply. Is that okay?"

A message is sent only if you say yes. We never use text messages to ask for permission to text you, and we never add a number that did not call the course.

Message frequency

One message per call you place. This is not a subscription or recurring program — there is no message series, and you will not hear from us again unless you contact the course again.

How to stop

Reply STOP to any message and you will be removed immediately and permanently. Reply HELP for help, or call the course directly. Message and data rates may apply. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
